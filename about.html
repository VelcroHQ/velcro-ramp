About Velcro

Digital payment infrastructure for the next generation of commerce.

Our Mission

Velcro was created to make digital asset conversion simple, frictionless, and completely automated. We believe individuals and businesses in Nigeria and across Africa should be able to move between fiat currency and cryptocurrencies in minutes, without counterparty anxiety or manual delays.

What We Do

We provide high-speed, programmatic onramp and offramp settlement services across major blockchain networks and local banking rails:

  • Instant Buy (Onramp): Pay via Nigerian bank transfer and receive USDT, USDC, BTC, ETH, or SOL in your wallet in under 3 minutes.
  • Instant Sell (Offramp): Send crypto to a dedicated deposit address and receive instant Naira payout to any Nigerian commercial bank.
  • Merchant Solutions: Businesses can deploy white-label crypto checkout pages with custom margins at merchant.usevelcro.com.

Security & Compliance

Velcro operates with trusted liquidity and custody providers including Switch and HostFi. We implement bank-grade encryption, automated reconciliation, and strict AML/KYC risk-monitoring protocols.
