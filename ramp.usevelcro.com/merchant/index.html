FAQ

Questions merchants ask us.

Do I hold my customers' money?

No. Each customer pays into a bank account made for their order, and crypto goes straight to their wallet (or naira straight to their bank when they sell). Your earnings collect in your Velcro balance.

How do I make money?

You set a markup on top of Velcro's price for each coin, separately for buying and selling. Your customers see one price, and the markup on every completed order goes to your balance.

Which coins can my customers trade?

BTC, ETH, SOL, USDT and USDC, bought with naira by bank transfer and sold for naira paid to any Nigerian bank.

Who verifies my customers?

You do. Velcro verifies your business before your store goes live, and the merchant terms make you responsible for knowing your customers. Check the rules that apply to your business with your own adviser.

Can I use my own domain?

Your store starts on a Velcro address you choose. Your own domain and your own subdomain on Velcro are coming next.

How long does approval take?

Usually within one working day. We'll email you as soon as your store is approved, or tell you what we need if something's missing.
