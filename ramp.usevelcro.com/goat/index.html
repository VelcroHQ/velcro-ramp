HostFi

                    
Crypto sells paid out by HostFi: BTC and ETH, plus SOL, USDT and USDC when switched on.

                

                

                    Checking…
                    
                

            


            

                
HostFi isn't connected yet

                
Add HOSTFI_CLIENT_ID and HOSTFI_CLIENT_SECRET to php-backend/.env on the server, then subscribe https://ramp.usevelcro.com/webhook/hostfi in the HostFi portal under Settings → Developers → Webhook Subscriptions. Until then no HostFi coins are shown to customers.

            


            

                

                    
Coins

                    
BTC and ETH are only sold through HostFi, so their switch hides the coin. SOL, USDT and USDC move from their current provider to HostFi when switched on.

                    

                        
                        
                        
                        
                        
                    

                    

                        

                            
                            
                        

                        

                            
                            
                        

                        

                            
                            
                        

                    

                    
The fee comes off the naira HostFi pays for each coin. The timer is how long a customer has to send; deposits that arrive later are still paid at the rate when they confirm.

                    
                


                

                    
Wallet balances

                    
Payouts come from the NGN wallet. Keep it funded above your largest expected sell.
