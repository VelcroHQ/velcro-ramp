Non-Custodial Order Execution

All transactions executed on this platform are strictly non-custodial:

  • Buy Orders (On-Ramp): You enter your personal destination cryptocurrency wallet address. An order-specific dynamic Nigerian virtual account number is created. Upon confirmed receipt of your fiat transfer via NIP, cryptocurrency is transferred directly from liquidity rails to your designated wallet.
  • Sell Orders (Off-Ramp): You provide your verified Nigerian bank account details and transfer cryptocurrency to an order-specific blockchain deposit address or QR code. Once confirmed on-chain, Nigerian Naira (NGN) is immediately credited to your bank account via automated NIP transfer.
Neither the Merchant nor Velcro holds your crypto assets or fiat in custodial balances. All transactions settle directly between verified Nigerian banking rails and your self-custody blockchain wallets.
