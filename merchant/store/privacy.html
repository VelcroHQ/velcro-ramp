Northline Exchange Privacy Policy

This page explains what information is used when you place an order with Northline Exchange, and how to contact us with a privacy question.

Storefront Operator: Northline Exchange
Order technology: Velcro Technologies Limited
Last updated: 12 November 2026
01

Who handles your information

This Exchange Desk runs the storefront and handles customer support. Velcro Technologies Limited provides the software and payment connections used to process orders.

  • The exchange operator: Manages this storefront and the support it provides to customers.
  • Velcro Technologies Limited: Provides the order software and connects orders to payment and crypto settlement providers. For questions about information handled by Velcro, email support@usevelcro.com.
02

Information used for an order

The information used depends on the order. It may include:

  • Contact and sign-in: Email address used to access your account and receive order updates;
  • Bank Settlement: Destination Nigerian bank account number, bank name, and account holder name (verified electronically via NIP name enquiry) for off-ramp payouts;
  • Cryptocurrency Details: Destination public wallet address, network selection, and on-chain transaction hashes;
  • Technical information: IP address, browser and device details, and records used to protect and troubleshoot the service.
We do not sell your order information to advertisers. Information may be shared with banks, payment providers and blockchain networks when needed to process an order or meet a legal requirement.
03

Why information is used

Information is used to provide the service you request, protect orders, respond to questions and meet legal requirements.

  • To create and process your order;
  • To confirm payments and crypto deposits;
  • To send order updates and respond to support requests; and
  • To investigate misuse and comply with legal obligations.
04

Security and retention

Access controls and other safeguards are used to protect information. No online service can guarantee complete security.

We keep order information while it is needed for support, transaction records, fraud prevention and legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information.

05

Your privacy choices

To ask about your information or request a correction, email support@usevelcro.com. We may need to keep some records to support an order or meet a legal requirement.

You also maintain the right to lodge a formal complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at https://ndpc.gov.ng.
