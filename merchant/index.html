FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

Do I hold or handle my customers' money?

No. Each customer pays into a dynamic virtual account generated strictly for their individual order. The crypto goes straight to their wallet (or naira straight to their bank account when selling). You never take custody of customer funds.

How do my profits accumulate and withdraw?

You set your custom markup percentage on top of base liquidity. When an order completes, your margin is credited immediately to your merchant balance. You can withdraw to any Nigerian bank account at any time with one click.

Which assets and blockchains are supported?

USDT (Solana, Base, TRC20, BEP20, Polygon), USDC (Solana, Base, Polygon, Ethereum), BTC (native), ETH (native, Base, Arbitrum), and SOL (native).

Can I use my own custom domain?

Yes! Every merchant immediately receives a dedicated subdomain (e.g. brand.usevelcro.com). You can also request a fully custom domain mapping directly through your dashboard settings.

How fast does business approval take?

Account approval is fast and automated, typically reviewed within a single working day. You will receive an immediate email confirmation the moment your exchange goes live.
