01 Scope & Role of Velcro

Velcro provides non-custodial, white-label cryptocurrency exchange infrastructure. By registering a merchant account, deploying a dedicated storefront, or connecting via API, you ("Operator" or "Merchant") enter into a binding operating agreement with Velcro ("Velcro", "we", "our").

Velcro acts solely as a technology and liquidity router. Velcro is not an investment adviser, banking institution, or custodial trustee. The Operator maintains direct relationship ownership with their customers.

02 Zero-Float & Non-Custodial Architecture

Velcro's infrastructure operates entirely on automated, non-custodial settlement rails:

  • Autonomous Inbound Routing: For every buy order, a single-use Nigerian bank virtual account is dynamically generated for the end customer. Customer fiat deposits are routed straight to automated settlement engines.
  • Direct Wallet Dispatch: Cryptocurrency purchased by the customer is delivered straight to the blockchain wallet address specified by the customer.
  • Direct Bank Off-Ramp: When a customer sells crypto, payout is dispatched directly to the customer's verified Nigerian commercial or microfinance bank account.
  • Zero Capital Lockup: The Operator is never required to provide capital float, liquidity deposits, or collateral reserves to process customer orders.
Important: Neither the Operator nor Velcro holds custody of customer fiat or crypto balances during standard exchange flows. Each transaction executes and settles atomically.

03 Merchant Verification & Customer Due Diligence (KYC)

Before launching a live exchange desk or accepting public volume, the Operator must successfully complete Velcro's business verification process, providing accurate corporate, business, and operational documentation.

Operator's Responsibility to Know Customers: As an independent exchange desk operator, you agree that you are solely responsible for knowing and screening your end customers in compliance with applicable anti-money laundering (AML), counter-terrorism financing (CFT), and consumer protection regulations in your operating jurisdiction.

04 Pricing, Markups & Revenue Settlement

Operators hold full discretion over their consumer pricing spreads:

  • Custom Markup Configuration: The Operator sets independent profit margins (percentages) per asset across both Buy and Sell sides in their merchant dashboard.
  • Unified Customer Pricing: End customers view a single, transparent, all-inclusive price. The Operator's configured margin is embedded seamlessly into the displayed exchange quote.
  • Accrued Earnings: Upon successful order completion, the Operator's markup is credited instantaneously to their merchant ledger balance.
  • Bank Withdrawals: Accrued merchant earnings may be withdrawn directly to the Operator's designated Nigerian bank account at any time, subject to standard automated banking verification.

05 Supported Assets & Network Rules

Velcro supports digital assets across verified liquidity providers, including USDT (Solana, Base, TRON, BSC, Polygon), USDC (Solana, Base, Polygon, Ethereum), Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and Solana (SOL).

Minimum transaction limits apply ($1 for EVM L2s and Solana; $5 for Ethereum Mainnet and TRON). Customers attempting transactions below minimum limits or sending mismatched blockchain assets bear sole liability for blockchain transaction fees or recovery constraints.
