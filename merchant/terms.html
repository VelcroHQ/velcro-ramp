01 What the service does

Velcro Merchant lets your business set up a branded page where customers can place crypto buy and sell orders. Velcro provides the dashboard and connects orders to payment, liquidity and blockchain providers.

You are responsible for your business, the information you publish, and the support you provide to your customers. These terms apply when you create or use a merchant account.

02 Orders and settlement

Customers follow the payment or wallet instructions shown for each order. Payment confirmation and blockchain processing depend on the relevant providers and networks.

  • For a buy, the customer transfers naira to the account details assigned to that order.
  • After payment is confirmed, the purchased asset is sent to the wallet address entered by the customer.
  • For a sell, the customer sends crypto to the order address; after confirmation, the naira payout is processed to the bank account on the order.
Order timing: Many standard orders finish within a few minutes after payment or deposit confirmation. Bitcoin often needs 10–60 minutes for a network confirmation, and banks or providers may take longer. Avoid promising a fixed arrival time.

04 Your prices and earnings

You set the markup for each supported asset in your dashboard. Customers see the resulting quote before they place an order.

  • Set separate markups for buy and sell orders where the dashboard allows it.
  • Completed orders appear in your dashboard, along with the earnings recorded for them.
  • Withdrawals are subject to the balance, account checks and limits shown in your dashboard.
