DATA PRIVACY & SECURITY PROTOCOLS

Velcro Merchant Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2026 · Information and telemetry practices for Velcro Merchant infrastructure

01 Overview & Data Principles

This Privacy Policy outlines how Velcro ("we", "us") collects, secures, and handles information relating to Operators ("Merchants") and transactional telemetry processed across white-label exchange storefronts.

Our guiding architecture is minimal data retention: we collect only what is strictly necessary to execute automated blockchain settlements, process Nigerian Interbank Settlement System (NIP) transfers, and maintain regulatory compliance.

02 Information We Collect

When operating a merchant account, we collect:

  • Merchant Profile Data: Operating name, trading name, email address, password hash, and payout Nigerian bank account numbers.
  • Storefront Branding Data: Logo assets, brand color specifications, custom subdomain preferences, and markup configurations.
  • Transactional Telemetry: Order IDs, amounts, crypto asset codes, destination wallet addresses, and NIP bank transfer reference identifiers.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser user-agents, and API request logs used exclusively for DDoS prevention, rate-limiting, and fraud monitoring.

03 How We Use Your Data

Collected information is used exclusively to:

  • Route automated order settlement between bank accounts and blockchain liquidity protocols.
  • Accrue and disburse merchant markup earnings to your verified bank account.
  • Dispatch automated order receipts and transactional updates to customers.
  • Detect, prevent, and report fraudulent activity, unauthorized account takeovers, or sanctions evasion.

We do not sell, rent, or trade merchant or customer data to marketing aggregators or third-party advertisers under any circumstances.

04 Data Security & Encryption

All data in transit is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Sensitive credentials and API tokens are hashed using cryptographic salt functions. Production database instances are isolated behind internal virtual private clouds with zero public exposure.

05 Data Retention & Rights

Transactional records are retained for the duration mandated by financial anti-money laundering regulations in Nigeria. Merchants may update their account information or request account closure at any time through their dashboard settings.

06 Contact

For data inquiries or privacy concerns, contact our Data Protection Team at support@usevelcro.com.
