Merchant privacy

Updated 12 November 2026 · For businesses using Velcro Merchant

This page explains what information Velcro Technologies Limited uses when a business creates or runs a merchant account and branded exchange.

01 Information we use

We use information about your business and its orders to provide the merchant dashboard, operate your exchange page, process requests and respond to support issues.

The information we need depends on the features you use and the orders placed by your customers.

02 Information We Collect

When operating a merchant account, we collect:

  • Merchant Profile Data: Operating name, trading name, email address, password hash, and payout Nigerian bank account numbers.
  • Storefront Branding Data: Logo assets, brand color specifications, custom subdomain preferences, and markup configurations.
  • Transactional Telemetry: Order IDs, amounts, crypto asset codes, destination wallet addresses, and NIP bank transfer reference identifiers.
  • Technical data: IP addresses, browser details and request records used to operate, troubleshoot and protect the service.

03 How We Use Your Data

We use this information to:

  • Provide your account, storefront and order history;
  • Calculate and display the prices you set;
  • Record earnings and process eligible withdrawal requests;
  • Send account and order updates; and
  • Investigate security issues and meet legal requirements.

We do not sell merchant account information to advertisers.

04 Keeping information safe

We use access controls and other safeguards to protect information. Access is limited to people and systems that need it to operate the service. No online service can promise that every system or transmission is completely secure.

05 Retention and your choices

We keep information for as long as it is needed to run your account, maintain order records, address disputes and meet legal requirements. You can update some account details in your dashboard or contact us about closing your account. We may need to keep certain records after closure.

06 Contact us

For a privacy question or request, email support@usevelcro.com.
