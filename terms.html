How an order works

Before you confirm an order, Convert shows the asset, quote, payment or deposit details, and any time limit that applies. Follow the instructions on that order screen.

Many orders finish within 1–3 minutes after payment or a crypto deposit is confirmed. Bitcoin network confirmation often takes 10–60 minutes. Bank and provider processing can add time.

Buying crypto:

  • Enter the wallet address and amount you want to buy;
  • Transfer the stated naira amount to the bank details shown for that order, before the payment window closes; and
  • Once the payment is confirmed, the purchased asset is sent to the wallet address you entered.

Selling crypto:

  • Enter the bank account where you want to receive naira;
  • Send the asset to the address shown, using the network specified on the order; and
  • After the network confirms the deposit, the naira payout is processed to your bank account.
Check before sending: Blockchain transfers are usually irreversible. A transfer sent to the wrong address or network, or without a required memo, may not be recoverable.
