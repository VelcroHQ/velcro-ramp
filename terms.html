8.1 Non-Custodial Architecture: Velcro operates as an autonomous, non-custodial technology rail. Velcro does not take custody of customer fiat funds, hold private keys, or maintain speculative trading accounts for retail users.

8.2 On-Ramp Execution (Buy Crypto):

  • You enter your destination blockchain wallet address and desired fiat amount;
  • The Platform dynamically issues a dedicated virtual bank account with a fixed payment window;
  • Upon receipt and automated confirmation of your NIP bank transfer, crypto is dispatched straight from institutional liquidity pools to your designated wallet address.

8.3 Off-Ramp Execution (Sell Crypto):

  • You supply your verified Nigerian bank account details (account number and bank name) and initiate a sell request;
  • The Platform generates a unique cryptographic deposit address or QR code on the selected network (e.g., Solana, Tron, Base, Bitcoin);
  • Upon required on-chain block confirmations, Velcro automatically triggers an instant NIP transfer to your verified Nigerian bank account.
