01

Scope and Data Controller

1.1 This Privacy Policy applies to personal data collected by Velcro Technologies Limited ("Velcro", "we", "us", or "our") through our website https://ramp.usevelcro.com, APIs, mobile interfaces, and white-label merchant exchange rails.

1.2 Data Controller: For the purposes of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 ("NDPA"), Velcro Technologies Limited, having its principal registered address at Wuye District, Abuja, Federal Capital Territory, Nigeria, is the Data Controller. You can contact our Data Protection Officers directly at support@usevelcro.com.

02

Core Principles of Data Processing

In accordance with Section 24 of the NDPA 2023, Velcro processes all personal data based on the following statutory principles:

  • Lawfulness, Fairness & Transparency: Processed legitimately and transparently without hidden tracking;
  • Purpose Limitation: Collected solely for specified, explicit, and legitimate transaction and compliance purposes;
  • Data Minimisation: Adequate, relevant, and limited to what is strictly necessary to execute exchange orders;
  • Accuracy: Maintained accurately and kept up to date;
  • Storage Limitation: Retained only for as long as required for order fulfillment or statutory compliance;
  • Integrity and Confidentiality: Safeguarded by advanced cryptographic encryption and multi-factor access barriers.
03

Information We Collect

3.1 Direct Information:

  • Contact & Authentication: Email address for passkey or one-time password (OTP) verification;
  • Banking & Settlement Data: Destination Nigerian bank account number, bank name, and account holder name (verified via NIP name enquiry) for off-ramp fiat credits;
  • Blockchain Data: Destination cryptocurrency wallet address, network type, and public transaction hashes;
  • Customer Communications: Support inquiries, transaction dispute details, or feedback submitted to our team.

3.2 Technical & Automated Telemetry:

  • IP addresses, browser type, device operating system, timestamp logs, and fraud prevention telemetry used to protect accounts against hijacking and bot exploitation.
04

Lawful Bases for Processing

We process your personal data under the lawful bases recognized by the NDPA 2023:

  • Contractual Performance: Necessary to generate dynamic virtual accounts, route payments, and dispatch cryptocurrency to your wallet;
  • Legal Obligation: Compliance with Nigerian anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorist financing (CFT) laws, NFIU reporting mandates, and financial record-keeping directives;
  • Legitimate Interests: Securing the Platform, preventing rate exploitation, mitigating chargeback fraud, and improving settlement speeds;
  • Consent: Where you have expressly consented to specific non-essential cookies or communications.
05

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data strictly to:

  • Automate on-ramp and off-ramp transactions via Nigerian Inter-Bank Settlement System (NIP) rails;
  • Verify destination Nigerian bank accounts before transferring funds;
  • Broadcast cryptocurrency distributions to appropriate blockchain validator nodes;
  • Deliver transaction confirmations and order tracking updates;
  • Prevent identity theft, payment fraud, and unauthorized platform manipulation;
  • Fulfill statutory reporting obligations to competent Nigerian regulatory agencies.
06

Sharing and Disclosures

No Sale of Data: Velcro never sells, rents, or monetizes your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

We disclose personal data solely to trusted partners necessary to fulfill transactions:

  • Settlement & Banking Partners: Licensed Nigerian commercial banks and payment switches powering dynamic virtual accounts and NIP transfers;
  • Liquidity Partners: Regulated exchange and liquidity rails (e.g., HostFi, PAJ, Switch) executing on-chain liquidity routing;
  • Public Blockchains: Public wallet addresses and transaction hashes broadcast to decentralized networks (e.g., Solana, Base, Bitcoin);
  • Regulatory Authorities: Competent Nigerian law enforcement bodies (e.g., NFIU, EFCC, SEC) where required by a lawful court order or statutory reporting mandate.
07

Cross-Border Data Transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside Nigeria for cloud infrastructure, distributed database caching, or blockchain validation, Velcro ensures compliance with Sections 41–43 of the NDPA 2023. We utilize standard contractual clauses, encryption at rest, and strict confidentiality agreements ensuring equivalent data protection.

08

Security Safeguards

Velcro maintains industry-standard technical, organizational, and physical security measures, including:

  • TLS 1.3 / AES-256 cryptographic encryption for all data in transit and at rest;
  • Passkey authentication and cryptographic signature validation;
  • Automated DDoS protection, web application firewalls, and real-time anomaly detection;
  • Strict role-based access controls limiting internal access to sensitive customer records.
09

Data Retention

We retain transaction records and associated customer identifiers for a minimum period of five (5) years in compliance with statutory Nigerian anti-money laundering and tax accounting regulations. Upon expiration of mandatory retention periods, data is permanently scrubbed or anonymized.

10

Your Statutory Rights under NDPA 2023

As a data subject in Nigeria, you possess fundamental legal rights, including:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data Velcro holds about you;
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete records;
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of data where retention is no longer legally mandated;
  • Right to Object: Object to processing carried out under legitimate interest;
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transmission of your data in a structured, commonly used electronic format.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please email our privacy team at support@usevelcro.com with the subject "Data Subject Request".

11

Cookies & Telemetry

We utilize strictly necessary session cookies and local storage tokens solely to maintain authenticated sessions, secure passkey verification, and remember theme preferences. We do not use third-party invasive behavioral tracking pixels.

12
