DATA PROTECTION NOTICE • NDPA 2023

Northline Exchange Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how your personal data is handled when you buy or sell digital assets through Northline Exchange, powered by Velcro Technologies Limited.

Storefront Operator: Northline Exchange
Infrastructure Processor: Velcro Technologies Limited
Statutory Law: Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023
Last Updated: April 2026
01

Dual Roles under the NDPA 2023

When you conduct transactions on this digital exchange storefront, your personal data is processed under a clear division of operational responsibilities:

  • The Storefront Merchant (This Exchange Desk): Functions as the retail customer relationship manager, setting markup rates and receiving order confirmations.
  • Velcro Technologies Limited: Acts as the autonomous infrastructure rail and data processor (having its registered office at Wuye District, Abuja, FCT, Nigeria; support@usevelcro.com), executing dynamic virtual account matching via NIP banking switches and on-chain crypto distribution.
02

Information We Process

We collect and process the minimum information required to complete your exchange orders securely:

  • Authentication: Email address for passkey or OTP session verification;
  • Bank Settlement: Destination Nigerian bank account number, bank name, and account holder name (verified electronically via NIP name enquiry) for off-ramp payouts;
  • Cryptocurrency Details: Destination public wallet address, network selection, and on-chain transaction hashes;
  • Security Logs: IP address, device telemetry, and anti-fraud timestamps to safeguard accounts from unauthorized tampering.
We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers. All data is processed strictly for transaction fulfillment, anti-fraud safeguards, and statutory Nigerian compliance.
03

Lawful Bases for Processing

Data processing is conducted pursuant to Section 25 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023:

  • Performance of Contract: To generate order-specific virtual bank accounts, match NIP payments, and broadcast cryptocurrency deliveries;
  • Legal Compliance: Adhering to statutory anti-money laundering (AML), counter-terrorist financing (CFT), and NFIU reporting mandates;
  • Legitimate Interests: Protecting the storefront against bot abuse, cyber attacks, and unauthorized payment recalls.
04

Security & Retention

Data in transit and at rest is protected by AES-256 encryption, TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols, and passkey authentication.

Transaction records are retained for a minimum statutory period of five (5) years in compliance with Nigerian financial regulations, after which they are securely scrubbed.

05

Your Data Subject Rights

Under the NDPA 2023, you have the right to request access to your personal data, rectify inaccurate records, request deletion where retention is no longer statutory, and lodge inquiries with our privacy team at support@usevelcro.com.

You also maintain the right to lodge a formal complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at https://ndpc.gov.ng.
