02

How orders are processed

Follow the payment and network instructions shown on your order:

  • Buying crypto: Enter your wallet address and pay the naira amount to the bank details assigned to your order. After the payment is confirmed, the crypto is sent to the address you entered.
  • Selling crypto: Enter your bank details and send the asset to the deposit address shown. After the network confirms it, the naira payout is processed to your bank.
Many orders finish within 1–3 minutes after payment or a crypto deposit is confirmed. Bitcoin network confirmation often takes 10–60 minutes. We submit eligible bank payouts after confirmation; your bank or provider may take longer to post them.
03

Check the details before sending

Check each order carefully before you pay or send crypto:

  • Crypto sent to the wrong address or network usually cannot be recovered.
  • Use the correct bank account and amount shown for that order.
  • Complete the transfer before the order timer expires. If it expires first, contact the exchange before sending money.

Bitcoin transfers commonly need 10–60 minutes for a network confirmation; other networks and banks can also take longer than expected.
