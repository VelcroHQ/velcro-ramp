    Help & Transparency

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about trading through this exchange desk.

    How fast will I receive my crypto or naira?
    Trades are fulfilled automatically through Velcro's institutional settlement engine. When buying crypto, coins are dispatched to your external wallet within 2 to 5 minutes after your naira bank transfer confirms. When selling crypto, naira is deposited directly into your Nigerian bank account once your blockchain transaction confirms.
    Do I need to deposit or leave funds on this platform?
    No. This platform is 100% non-custodial. You never store funds or risk your balance here. Every transaction executes directly between your verified Nigerian bank account and your personal external crypto wallet.
    How are exchange rates determined?
    Rates update in real-time based on live institutional OTC market liquidity. The price you see on your order ticket is guaranteed and locked during your checkout window, with zero hidden spreads or surprise charges.
    How are my funds protected during bank transfer?
    All bank transfers are secured by dedicated automated escrow accounts provided by regulated banking partners. You never transfer money to random personal accounts or P2P middlemen; your payment is tied to your specific order and verified automatically.
