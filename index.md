# Velcro Convert — Instant Crypto Onramp & Offramp for Nigeria

> Buy, sell, and swap cryptocurrencies (USDT, USDC, BTC, ETH, SOL) with Nigerian Naira (NGN) bank transfers in under 3 minutes.

## Overview
Velcro is a non-custodial crypto payment gateway and digital asset conversion service. Every transaction is settled automatically through high-speed banking rails and native blockchain contracts.

- **Instant Buy:** Enter amount in Naira (NGN), transfer to an automated virtual bank account, and receive crypto directly into your self-custody wallet.
- **Instant Sell:** Send crypto to a dedicated on-chain deposit address and receive an automated Naira bank payout to any verified Nigerian bank account.
- **Merchant Storefronts:** Provision branded exchange widgets or subdomains (`{brand}.usevelcro.com`) with custom fee structures.
